Digital Health Hackathon 2026  |  Technology One-Pager 
Digitally-Enabled Adherence Platform (DEAP) 
Principal Investigator: Avani Modi, PhD; David Hooper, MD; Charles Varnell, MD 
The Problem 
Not taking medication as prescribed is one of the biggest hidden drivers of poor health outcomes, and it hits 
young transplant patients especially hard. For an adolescent with a kidney transplant, missed 
immunosuppressant doses can lead to organ rejection, hospitalization, and death. At Cincinnati Children’s, 
nearly half of kidney transplant patients and caregivers report at least one barrier to taking their medication, 
and those who do are nearly three times more likely to experience acute rejection. For a transplant family, a 
missed dose is not a minor lapse; it can mean rejection of a donated organ or a life-threatening event. Yet 
barriers are rarely assessed in a structured way, and there is no standard method to deliver proven strategies 
to help patients overcome them between visits. 
Current Stage 
DEAP is built on the team's clinic-based expertise, which was shown to reduce kidney transplant rejection 
episodes while actually saving money. DEAP is software-only, with no expensive proprietary hardware, 
connecting instead to the communication tools patients already use, and it personalizes the timing and 
content of messages to each patient’s barriers, cognitive load, language, and schedule. The clinical foundation 
is proven; the opportunity now is the experience that carries it to families at scale. 
Jumping Off Points for Hackathon Teams 
The opportunity is the patient-and-family-facing experience layer on top of the evidence-based clinical 
content. Possible starting points: 
• 
A barrier-assessment and onboarding experience that helps a patient or caregiver quickly identify 
what is getting in the way 
• 
A personalization layer that adapts message timing and content to a patient’s situation and 
preferences 
• 
Engagement and re-engagement features: reminders, nudges, streaks, progress visualization 
• 
A patient-facing dashboard that makes adherence progress easy to see 
• 
A clinician-facing view showing which patients need attention and which interventions work 
• 
Modern visual design, accessibility, and mobile experience 
Things to Consider, or Not! 
• 
The evidence-based clinical content and adherence strategies stay as is. The opportunity is the 
experience layer that delivers them 
• 
A self-contained prototype with sample data is a strong one-day target; no integration with live patient 
data is needed 
• 
Choose the surface that fits your team’s strengths. You do not need to build all of it 
Why Choose This Project 
You will work on a platform built on a clinic-proven program that reduced organ rejection while saving money, 
and build the experience that helps it reach families every day. The problem is enormous (about half of patients 
with chronic conditions do not take medications as prescribed, at a cost of roughly $528 billion and 125,000 
lives a year), and the surface is squarely consumer-facing: messaging, personalization, dashboards, and 
engagement. The team is engaged and available the morning of the event to brief teams and answer questions.
