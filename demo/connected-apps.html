Reminders reach families where they already are

Each family picks how check-ins reach them: text, calendar, MyChart, email, phone reminders. Every answer lands in one list for the care team. Routine "it's working" replies are logged automatically. Problems go to a named person with the plan attached.

Working in this demo

  • Channel picker with privacy defaults
  • Check-in answers from any channel feed one worklist
  • "Working" replies are logged without a staff task
  • "Not working" and "Need help" go to a named owner with the plan attached

Simulated

  • All patients, staff, dates and messages are fictional
  • No texts, emails or reminders are actually sent
  • No live MyChart, Apple Health or Alexa connection
  • Previews are our own mockups, not real app screens

What we don't claim

  • A "taken" reply is patient-reported, not verified adherence
  • No partnership with any company; the design is ready to partner
  • MAPS outcomes belong to the clinical program, not this prototype
  • Spanish and Arabic need approved clinical wording
